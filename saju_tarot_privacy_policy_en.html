AI Tarot - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 | Version: 1.0.0

🔒 Privacy Summary
The 'AI Tarot' app does not collect or transmit your written questions, card selections, or personal settings to any external developer server. All operations and stored data are processed locally on your device (On-Device).

1. Information Handled & Purpose

The 'AI Tarot' app uses the following data locally to provide tarot readings and AI fortune services:

  • User Inputs: Question text, chosen tarot cards, spread configurations, and app language preference.
  • Purpose: Interpreting tarot spreads, automatic topic classification, and providing on-device AI readings and reading history.
  • Storage: All questions and reading records are saved only in the device's local storage (SharedPreferences) and are never uploaded to external servers.

2. Data Retention Period

  • Locally stored reading records remain on your device until you manually delete them or uninstall the application.
  • No user registration is required, so no personal data exists on any external server.

3. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

To support free service operations, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic logs to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's policies.
  • Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required for serving AdMob advertisements, sharing results, and opening external links.
  • CAMERA: Used only when taking a photo for palm reading analysis (images are processed locally and never transmitted).
  • STORAGE: Used to maintain local app preferences and reading history.

5. User Rights & Data Control

  • Users can delete reading records anytime using the in-app clear/delete feature.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children or any users.
