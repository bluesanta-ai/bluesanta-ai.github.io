In short. STEP DWG Viewer runs entirely on your device. We operate no server, we hold no account for you, and your drawings are never uploaded to us. The only data that leaves your device does so because you asked for it — signing in to a cloud storage service you chose, or sharing an image yourself — with one exception: the banner ads in the app are served by Google, which collects the limited data described below.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android application STEP DWG Viewer (package com.bx.app.occt), published by KRSTE ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with your information and what it does not.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, location, photos or microphone input. We do not run analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of our own, and we do not build a profile of you.

The CAD files and drawings you open are never transmitted to us. We have no server that could receive them.

3. Information processed on your device

The app stores the following in its own private storage area on your device. None of it is sent anywhere.

WhatWhy
A copy of the files you open Drawings you choose are copied into the app's private folder so the viewer can read them, and so your original file is never modified.
Thumbnails and prepared geometry Cached so the recent-files list has previews and so reopening a large assembly is fast.
Recent files list File names, sizes and the time you last opened them.
Cloud sign-in tokens Stored encrypted (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences) so you do not have to sign in again each time. Never transmitted to us.
Display preferences Background, display mode and similar view settings.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • Storage access (all-files access on Android 11 and later, or a folder you grant) — used solely to find and open the drawings you select. The app reads the file types it supports; it does not scan, index or transmit anything else.
  • Internet and network state — used to reach the cloud storage service you sign in to, and to load advertisements.

You can refuse storage access and still use the app through the system file picker. Refusing simply limits the built-in browser.

5. Cloud storage connections

If you choose to sign in to Dropbox, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business or a WebDAV server, the app talks to that service directly from your device. Your credentials are entered on the provider's own sign-in page (or, for WebDAV, sent only to the server address you typed) and are never seen by us.

We request read-only access. The app lists folders and downloads the file you pick. It cannot upload, modify or delete anything in your cloud account.

Signing out inside the app deletes the stored tokens from your device. You can also revoke access from the provider's own account settings at any time.

6. Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system version and interactions with the ad. This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob partner apps

You can limit ad personalisation on your device under Settings → Google → Ads, or reset your advertising ID there.

7. Images you save or share

When you capture the current view, the image is saved to your device's gallery. When you share it, it goes to the app you choose. We receive no copy in either case.

8. Children

This app is a technical tool for viewing engineering drawings. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Deleting your data

  • Sign out of a cloud service in the app to delete its stored tokens.
  • Remove entries from the recent-files list, with or without deleting the copied file.
  • Clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → STEP DWG Viewer → Storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about the app: support@example.com

5. 클라우드 저장소 연결

Dropbox, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, WebDAV 서버에 로그인하기로 선택하면, 앱은 해당 서비스와 기기에서 직접 통신합니다. 인증 정보는 각 서비스의 로그인 화면에 입력되며(WebDAV의 경우 사용자가 입력한 서버 주소로만 전송됩니다), 저희는 이를 볼 수 없습니다.

요청하는 권한은 읽기 전용입니다. 앱은 폴더 목록을 불러오고 사용자가 고른 파일을 내려받습니다. 클라우드 계정에 무엇을 올리거나 수정하거나 삭제할 수 없습니다.

앱에서 로그아웃하면 저장된 토큰이 기기에서 삭제됩니다. 각 서비스의 계정 설정에서 언제든지 앱 연결을 해제할 수도 있습니다.

기기의 설정 → Google → 광고에서 맞춤 광고를 제한하거나 광고 ID를 초기화할 수 있습니다.

5. クラウドストレージとの連携

Dropbox、OneDrive、OneDrive for Business、WebDAV サーバーへのログインを選択した場合、アプリは そのサービスと端末から直接通信します。認証情報は各サービス自身のログイン画面で 入力され(WebDAV の場合はお客様が入力したサーバーアドレスにのみ送信されます)、当方が目にすることは ありません。

要求する権限は読み取り専用です。アプリはフォルダ一覧を取得し、選択されたファイルを ダウンロードします。クラウドアカウント内のデータをアップロード・変更・削除することはできません。

アプリ内でログアウトすると、保存されたトークンは端末から削除されます。各サービスのアカウント設定 からいつでも連携を解除することもできます。

6. 広告について

アプリは Google AdMob を通じてバナー広告を表示します。広告の配信と効果測定の ために、Google は端末の広告 ID、IP アドレス、端末モデル、OS バージョン、広告との操作などの情報を 収集する場合があります。この処理は、Google が自社のポリシーに基づき独立した管理者として行います。

  • Google プライバシーポリシー
  • AdMob パートナーアプリの情報を Google が使用する方法

端末の設定 → Google → 広告から、広告のパーソナライズを制限したり広告 ID をリセット したりできます。
